## 1. Governing rule

Every source document repeats the same constraint: **agents do not call each other's code directly.** Cross-agent communication happens only through governed shared database tables (signals, risk alerts, evidence links) or through a human approval step. This is stated explicitly for Delivery (`01.`), Workforce (`03.`), and Governance (`04.`).

## 4. Per-agent summary — what each agent needs, and from whom

**Delivery Performance (01)** needs: quality drift/rework context from Quality Intelligence, workforce-imbalance alerts from Workforce & Capability, and (optionally) mitigation patterns from Operational Knowledge. It owns milestones, throughput, delivery confidence, risk alerts, bottlenecks — nothing here is supplied by another agent.

**Quality Intelligence (02)** needs: SOPs/lessons/calibration content from Operational Knowledge for root-cause recommendations, daily throughput logs from Delivery Performance, and weekly workforce allocation data from Workforce & Capability (both feed the workload/fatigue root-cause hypothesis). It is still the most connected agent overall — alongside those three inbound reads, it pushes signals out to Delivery, Client Interaction, Workforce, and Governance.

**Client Interaction (05)** needs: throughput/risk data from Delivery, sanitized quality summaries from Quality Intelligence, aggregated workforce confidence from Workforce, and approved SOP context from Knowledge. Every draft it produces is gated by Delivery Manager approval — it can never publish autonomously.

**Workforce & Capability (03)** needs: skill-gap signals from Quality Intelligence and training documents from Operational Knowledge. It is designed to be independently buildable — it does not need the other agents finished first.

**Project Governance (04)** needs: quality escalations from Quality Intelligence and read-only risk alerts from Delivery. It explicitly must not duplicate delivery confidence scoring, quality drift detection, or workforce overload logic — it only aggregates and escalates.

**Operational Knowledge (06)** needs: structured operational data (projects, milestones, quality snapshots, risk alerts) for context, but its primary inputs are unstructured — SOPs, charters, escalation notes, lessons learned. It supplies all other agents but consumes signals from none of them directly (Quality writes lessons to it, which it then serves back).

## 5. Phase caveat

Only Delivery Performance, Quality Intelligence, and Client Interaction are Phase‑1/MVP. Workforce & Capability, Project Governance, and Operational Knowledge are Phase 2+ roadmap items — several of the edges above (5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12) describe *planned* integrations that depend on Phase 2 agents existing, not live MVP behavior. The Quality Intelligence roadmap doc confirms edges 1, 3, 4, 6/7 (signal emission to Delivery/Client/Workforce, OKA read) are implemented in code as of Phase 1.5/2.0; edge 8 (Governance escalation) is explicitly deferred to Phase 2.5.
